45 Mins 4 Servings
Main Course

Truffle Mushroom Risotto

Prep Time 15 Mins
Cook Time 30 Mins
Yields 4 Servings

The Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups Arborio rice
  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth, warmed
  • 1 cup wild mushrooms (cremini, shiitake), sliced
  • 1 tbsp black truffle oil

Method of Preparation

  1. 1. In a heavy saucepan, heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil over medium heat. Add the finely chopped shallots and sauté until translucent.
  2. 2. Stir in the Arborio rice, toasting it slightly for about 2 minutes. Pour in white wine and stir continuously until fully absorbed.
  3. 3. Begin adding the warm broth one ladle at a time, allowing the liquid to absorb before adding the next, stirring frequently.
